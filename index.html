03 / A closer look

From prototype to research.

My Honors research extended our wildfire-detection capstone, asking what it would take for a LoRa network to work outside a controlled demonstration.

Honors Thesis · 2026

Theoretical Feasibility Evaluation of LoRa Communication for Wildfire Detection Networks

Successfully defended · August 2026

OSU Capstone Showcase

From a working network to a defensible model

  1. Reach: propagation and link-budget calculations at 915 MHz.
  2. Reliability: packet delivery and multi-hop route success.
  3. Responsiveness: radio airtime, reporting intervals, and latency.
  4. Energy: radio power in context, with the MQ-2 heater dominating system demand.
