03 / Honors Research

From prototype to research.

Honors Thesis · 2026

Theoretical Feasibility Evaluation of LoRa Communication for Wildfire Detection Networks

My Honors thesis extended the wildfire-detection capstone by evaluating whether LoRa communication could realistically support a distributed wildfire sensor network under practical environmental and network conditions.

The analysis focused on communication range, packet reliability, latency, and energy consumption while examining the effects of vegetation, relay links, transmission timing, and sensor-node power requirements.

Read Honors Thesis PDF

Key engineering questions

  1. Range — How does propagation loss change when LoRa moves from open-space testing into vegetation and forest environments?
  2. Reliability — How does end-to-end packet delivery change as relay links are added?
  3. Latency — Does LoRa airtime or the sensor reporting interval dominate response time?
  4. Energy — Which components dominate the sensor node's total power requirement?
